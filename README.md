# 📋 China Factory On-Site Audit & Verification Checklist (Guangdong Edition)

> An open-source, practical field checklist designed for international B2B buyers, Amazon FBA sellers, and independent brands importing hardware, appliances, and consumer goods from China's Pearl River Delta manufacturing hubs (Guangzhou, Foshan, Dongguan, Shenzhen, Zhongshan).

Maintained by **[Mr. Tan](https://www.mrtan.net/)** — Independent boots-on-the-ground sourcing agent and dropshipping fulfillment provider based in Foshan, Guangdong.

---

## Pre-Audit: Online Background Verification
Before sending anyone to the factory floor, verify these critical business legitimacy markers:
- [ ] **Unified Social Credit Code (USCC):** Cross-check the business license on the official National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System (GSXT).
- [ ] **Registered Business Scope:** Ensure the company is legally registered as a manufacturer (`生产/制造`), not merely a trading intermediary (`批发/贸易`).
- [ ] **Export & Quality Certifications:** Verify CE, RoHS, UL, or ISO certificates directly with the issuing testing lab, rather than accepting unverified supplier PDFs.

---

## Phase 1: On-Site Facility & Production Line Check
- [ ] **Factory Name Match:** Confirm the physical factory gate and signage strictly match the legal company name on the business license.
- [ ] **Raw Material Inventory:** Inspect the raw material warehouse to verify standard industrial grades are used.
- [ ] **Machinery Ownership & Capacity:** Verify the production line is active, functional, and actually producing the target items rather than outsourcing to hidden sub-tier workshops.
- [ ] **Subcontracting Red Flags:** Inquire directly about proprietary component assembly vs. third-party outsourced assembly.

---

## Phase 2: Pre-Shipment Quality Control (QC)
- [ ] **AQL Standard Sampling:** Follow ISO 2859-1 (AQL 2.5 / 4.0) sampling plans to inspect carton drop tests, cosmetic defects, and mechanical functions.
- [ ] **Carton & Drop Resistance:** Test corrugated master cartons (standard 5-ply K=K or double-wall) against maritime transit humidity and long-haul shipping compression.
- [ ] **Blind Fulfillment Verification:** Ensure no domestic Chinese supplier invoices, unverified flyers, or supplier contact tags are left inside retail packaging.
